Why wire geometry matters

Each battery has its own cable model. Resistance is temperature corrected and uses the full positive/negative loop. Inductance is geometry-derived for a two-wire pair or can be entered directly for busbars/custom layouts.

Numerical simulation

Protection thresholds

Research rule: the solver can be made numerically finer than MATLAB defaults, but numerical resolution is not the same as physical truth. Cell-specific accuracy ultimately comes from measured HPPC/EIS/thermal data. HyperGrid keeps provenance and assumptions visible instead of hiding them.
